# Operación end-to-end con curl\n\nTodas las llamadas requieren `X-Tenant-Id`. El tenant default se siembra\nen V1 con UUID `00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000001`.\n\n```bash\nexport BASE=http://localhost:8080\nexport TENANT=00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000001\nexport H1="X-Tenant-Id: $TENANT"\nexport H2="Content-Type: application/json"\n```\n\n## 1. Setup mínimo (una vez)\n\n```bash\nPROJECT=$(uuidgen)\nPARTY=$(uuidgen)\nCONTRACT=$(uuidgen)\n\npsql -h localhost -U postgres -d construction <<EOF\nINSERT INTO project (tenant_id, project_id, name, timezone, status)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', '$PROJECT', 'Piloto', 'America/Mexico_City', 'ACTIVE');\n\nINSERT INTO party (tenant_id, party_id, legal_name)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', '$PARTY', 'Subcontratista SA de CV');\n\nINSERT INTO contract (tenant_id, contract_id, project_id, amount, currency,\n                      contract_type, effective_from)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', '$CONTRACT', '$PROJECT', 100000, 'USD', 'AIA_A201', CURRENT_DATE);\nEOF\n```\n\n## 2. Alta y verificación de compliance\n\n```bash\n# Crear requisito (queda PENDIENTE)\nREQ=$(curl -s -X POST $BASE/api/compliance/requirements           -H "$H1" -H "$H2"           -d "{\"subcontractorId\":\"$PARTY\",\"projectId\":\"$PROJECT\",\\n       \"tipoRequisito\":\"SEGURO_RC\",\"fechaVencimiento\":\"2025-12-31\"}"           | jq -r .requisitoId)\n\n# Verificar (SUBIR DOCUMENTO PRIMERO; aquí solo verificamos)\ncurl -s -X POST $BASE/api/compliance/requirements/$REQ/verify           -H "$H1" -H "$H2"           -d '{"documentoId":"00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000099"}'\n\n# Verificar head VIGENTE\npsql -h localhost -U postgres -d construction -c           "SELECT estado_global, version_actual FROM compliance_subcontractor_head \\n   WHERE subcontractor_id = '$PARTY';"\n```\n\n## 3. PO + recepción + factura\n\n```bash\npsql -h localhost -U postgres -d construction <<EOF\nINSERT INTO purchase_order (tenant_id, po_id, project_id, po_number, status)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', gen_random_uuid(), '$PROJECT', 'PO-1', 'OPEN');\n\nINSERT INTO purchase_order_line (tenant_id, po_line_id, po_id, line_number,\n                                 description, quantity, unit_price)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', gen_random_uuid(),\n        (SELECT po_id FROM purchase_order WHERE po_number='PO-1' AND tenant_id='$TENANT'),\n        1, 'Item', 10, 100);\n\nINSERT INTO goods_receipt (tenant_id, receipt_id, po_id, received_at, received_by)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', gen_random_uuid(),\n        (SELECT po_id FROM purchase_order WHERE po_number='PO-1' AND tenant_id='$TENANT'),\n        now(), '$PARTY');\n\nINSERT INTO goods_receipt_line (tenant_id, receipt_line_id, receipt_id, po_line_id, quantity_received)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', gen_random_uuid(),\n        (SELECT receipt_id FROM goods_receipt WHERE tenant_id='$TENANT' LIMIT 1),\n        (SELECT po_line_id FROM purchase_order_line WHERE tenant_id='$TENANT' LIMIT 1),\n        10);\n\nINSERT INTO invoice (tenant_id, invoice_id, project_id, contract_id, subcontractor_id,\n                     invoice_number, amount, currency, invoice_date, received_at, status, po_reference)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', gen_random_uuid(), '$PROJECT', '$CONTRACT', '$PARTY',\n        'INV-1', 1000, 'USD', CURRENT_DATE, now(), 'RECEIVED', 'PO-1');\n\nINSERT INTO invoice_line (tenant_id, invoice_line_id, invoice_id, line_number,\n                          description, quantity, unit_price)\nVALUES ('$TENANT', gen_random_uuid(),\n        (SELECT invoice_id FROM invoice WHERE invoice_number='INV-1' AND tenant_id='$TENANT'),\n        1, 'Item', 10, 100);\nEOF\n```\n\n## 4. Someter pago -> proceso BPMN\n\n```bash\nINVOICE=$(psql -h localhost -U postgres -d construction -t -A -c           "SELECT invoice_id FROM invoice WHERE invoice_number='INV-1' AND tenant_id='$TENANT'")\n\ncurl -s -X POST $BASE/api/payments/applications           -H "$H1" -H "$H2"           -d "{\"invoiceId\":\"$INVOICE\",\"subcontractorId\":\"$PARTY\",\\n       \"projectId\":\"$PROJECT\",\"monto\":1000,\"contractId\":\"$CONTRACT\"}"           | jq\n```\n\nRespuesta: `{"processInstanceId": "..."}`. El proceso `paymentApprovalProcess`\ncorre en Camunda (`http://localhost:8080/camunda/`).\n\n## 5. Revocación en vuelo (bloqueo estructural)\n\nRepite los pasos 2 y 4 para tener una factura en vuelo, luego:\n\n```bash\ncurl -s -X POST $BASE/api/compliance/requirements/$REQ/revoke           -H "$H1" -H "$H2"           -d '{"motivo":"Incumplimiento OSHA 1926.501","effectiveDate":"2025-01-15"}'\n```\n\nLa revocación correlaciona `ComplianceRevokedMessage` a cualquier instancia\nactiva. El `boundaryEvent` cancela el subproceso. El pago NO se ejecuta.\n\n## 6. Invalidación + compensación\n\n```bash\nPAYMENT=$(psql -h localhost -U postgres -d construction -t -A -c           "SELECT payment_id FROM payment WHERE invoice_id='$INVOICE'")\n\ncurl -s -X POST $BASE/api/payments/$PAYMENT/invalidate           -H "$H1" -H "$H2"           -d "{\"invalidityType\":\"CERTIFICADO_VENCIDO_RETROACTIVO\",\\n       \"evidenceId\":\"$(uuidgen)\",\"amount\":1000,\\n       \"contractualBasis\":\"Cláusula 15.2\",\\n       \"effectiveDate\":\"2024-12-01\",\\n       \"detectedBy\":\"$PARTY\"}"\n```\n\nCadena de eventos:\n1. `payment_invalidation` insertada\n2. `PaymentInvalidatedEvent` publicado\n3. `PaymentInvalidatedEventHandler.on()` -> `CompensationService.open()`\n4. `compensation_case` creada (si no existía)\n5. `CompensationCaseCreatedEvent` publicado\n6. `CompensationCaseCreatedHandler.on()` -> message correlation BPMN\n7. `compensationCaseProcess` arranca, aplica deducción, marca `RECOVERED`\n\n## 7. Health y verificación de invariantes\n\n```bash\n# Estado del sistema\ncurl -s $BASE/actuator/health | jq\n\n# Métricas clave\ncurl -s $BASE/actuator/metrics/billing.payments.executed | jq\ncurl -s $BASE/actuator/metrics/outbox_dlq_count | jq\n\n# Invariantes en BD\npsql -h localhost -U postgres -d construction <<'EOF'\n-- Cadena de auditoría no rota\nSELECT count(*) AS broken FROM (\n  SELECT a.sequence_number FROM audit_event a\n    LEFT JOIN audit_event prev ON prev.tenant_id = a.tenant_id\n         AND prev.sequence_number = a.sequence_number - 1\n   WHERE a.prev_hash IS DISTINCT FROM coalesce(prev.event_hash, '')\n) x;\n\n-- Sin outbox DLQ\nSELECT count(*) FROM projection_outbox WHERE status = 'DEAD_LETTERED';\n\n-- Sin recovery_account negativo (constraint CHECK ya lo impide)\nSELECT count(*) FROM recovery_account WHERE saldo_pendiente < 0;\n\n-- Sin progress CERTIFIED bajo threshold\nSELECT count(*) FROM progress_observation\n  WHERE status = 'CERTIFIED' AND progress_ratio_lower < contractual_threshold;\nEOF\n```\n\n## 8. Órdenes de fallo esperados (fail-loud)\n\n| Acción | Fallo esperado |\n|--------|----------------|\n| `POST /api/payments/applications` con `subcontractorId` sin compliance head | `500 IllegalStateException: Sin proyección de compliance` |\n| `POST /api/bids/packages/{id}/award` sin `justification` | `400 IllegalArgumentException: Adjudicación sin justificación` |\n| `POST /api/permits` con jurisdicción sin config | `500 IllegalStateException: Sin configuración de plazo` |\n| `POST /api/safety/incidents` con `occurredAt` futuro | `400 IllegalArgumentException: occurredAt no puede ser futuro` |\n| Cualquier request sin `X-Tenant-Id` | `400 Falta header X-Tenant-Id` |\n| Cualquier request con `X-Tenant-Id` que no es UUID | `400 X-Tenant-Id no es UUID` |\n\nEsto es el contrato de operación. Si algo falla silencioso, es un bug.\n\n## Revisión de hardening (candidata, no certificación)\n\nAntes de cambios de código ejecuta:\n\n```bash\npython scripts/verify-hardening-static.py\nbash scripts/prove-construction-niche.sh\nbash scripts/verify-invariants.sh <tenant-uuid>\nbash scripts/verify-gates.sh\n```\n\nLos dos primeros comandos son verificaciones estáticas/estructurales. Los dos últimos requieren `psql`, conexión a la base correcta y credenciales con permisos suficientes; sin conexión no hay prueba de RLS, invariantes ni despliegue BPMN. Para levantar cualquier ambiente real se necesita `mvn clean verify` y las pruebas de PostgreSQL/Testcontainers. Consultar `HARDENING_NOTES.md` y no aprobar GO-LIVE mientras existan los bloqueadores P0/P1 ahí descritos.
